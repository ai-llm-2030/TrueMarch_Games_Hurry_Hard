Marie-Claude explainsHow to play

  1. Aim — point the broom: Marie-Claude holds it where you want the stone to go. ←→ or the mouse.
  2. Turn — ↻ in-turn curls right, ↺ out-turn curls left. Aim to the side and let it curl in.
  3. Weight — hold, and let go in the band you want: Guard · Draw · Tap · Hit · Peel. Too light is hogged (removed).
  4. Sweep! — scrub fast in front of the stone: it runs further and straighter. Stop and it curls. Listen for "HURRY HARD!" — and "Whoa!" means stop.
  5. Score — after 10 stones, the side closest to the button scores one for every stone closer than the other side's best.
  6. Free-guard zone — the first 3 stones of an end may not knock a guard (between the hog line and the house) out of play.
  7. The pebble wears — where stones have run, the ice gets faster and swingier. The strip at the edge shows the wear.
